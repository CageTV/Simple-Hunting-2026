Simple Hunting Overhaul 2026 1.1.0. Install over 1.0.2. The Nexus package is the DLL; the Optimised Scripts add-on is GitHub only.

New in 1.1.0:
- Works with Hunterborn. With Hunterborn installed the optional scripts leave pelts and meat to Hunterborn (no second skinning behind its prompt). With Simple Hunterborn (Nexus 109288) also installed, Simple Hunting Overhaul runs the hunt and the scripts carry that mod's harvest-level and tier changes, so Hunterborn's loot follows your hunting experience.
- Switching Immersive Hunting Animations off on the Animations page now also takes its perk away directly, so it stops acting even when that mod's own script cannot load.
- The DLL (new and older builds) is 1.1.0.
